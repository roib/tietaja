# burned. A tale for young players: Mummo sends Vilja to find the fox and always says what

# Jussi and a little pattern to finish. The file format is described at the top of
# story.txt.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- defaults

node default_look
  Vilja: Nothing special about it.

node default_take
  Vilja: I can't carry that.

node default_use
  Vilja: That doesn't do anything.

node default_talk
  Vilja: It doesn't answer. Out here, even the wind keeps quiet.

node default_use_item
  Vilja: Hmm, not there. Mummo will know what to do.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the camp

node intro
  Vilja: Three nights now, and not one flicker in the sky.
  Vilja: Mummo sent word that the fox has stopped running. I'd better ask her what's wrong.

node look_kota
  Vilja: A kota of birch poles and turf, half buried in snow. Smoke curls out of the top, smelling of birch and dried fish.

node take_kota
  Vilja: It's a house, Vilja.

node use_kota
  Vilja: It's warm in there, but the fox won't be.

node look_snowshoes
  Vilja: Snowshoes, leaning against the kota. Birch frames laced with reindeer sinew.

node take_snowshoes
  Mummo: Those are mine, child! Come and talk to me first.

node look_band
  Vilja: A band of red and white wool, woven with little figures: a bear, a hare, an owl.
  Vilja: A bear, a hare, an owl. A bear, a hare, an owl. A bear...
  Vilja: ...and there the weave is frayed away. What comes after the bear?

node take_band
  Mummo: Leave that be. My mother wove it, and her mother taught her.

node look_fire
  Vilja: Mummo's fire. Birch logs, burning low and steady. The only warm light for miles.

node use_fire
  Vilja: I hold my hands to it for a moment. Better.

node take_fire
  Vilja: Not with my bare hands. Mummo gave me an ember in a box.

node look_reindeer
  [!sky_lit] Vilja: Mummo's herd, huddled together in the dark. They keep lifting their heads to sniff the wind.
  [sky_lit] Vilja: The reindeer stand in the green light, calm again.

node take_reindeer
  Vilja: I think it would object.

node talk_reindeer
  Vilja: Hush now. The lights will come back. I promise.

node look_mummo
  Vilja: Mummo has herded reindeer on these fells for sixty winters. She keeps glancing up at the empty sky.

node take_mummo
  Mummo: Ha! I'm not a sack of flour.

node talk_mummo
  [!knows_fox] jump mummo_tale
  Mummo: Well, child?
  [!puukko] Mummo: Go into the forest first and talk to Jussi the trapper. He knows where the fox is.
  [puukko !took_fur] Mummo: Now up to the fell top, past Jussi's cabin. Look for the fox's fur, caught in the snare.
  [took_fur !fox_out] Mummo: Take the fur to the fox's den in the rocks. It will come out when it smells its own fur.
  [fox_out !fox_free] Mummo: Cut the wire off the fox's leg with Jussi's puukko. Gently, now.
  [fox_free !fox_lit] Mummo: Warm its tail with the ember in my box.
  [fox_lit !sky_lit] Mummo: Show the fox its way, the way my band tells it: after the bear comes the hare, and then the owl.
  choice What's the woven band? -> mummo_band
  choice I'll go. -> end

node mummo_tale
  Mummo: Vilja! You came. Sit by the fire, quick. You've seen it? The sky?
  Vilja: It's so dark. Not one light, the whole way here.
  Mummo: Every winter night, a fox runs across the fells, and its tail of fire flicks sparks up into the sky.
  Mummo: The sparks are the northern lights. Revontulet, the fox fires.
  Mummo: But three nights ago the fox stopped running. Something has hurt it.
  Mummo: Find the fox, child, and help it. Go into the forest first: Jussi the trapper will know where it is.
  Mummo: Here: my snowshoes, for the deep snow on the fell.
  give snowshoes
  Mummo: And an ember from my fire, in a birch-bark box. If the fox is cold, it will need fire.
  give ember
  Mummo: Come back and ask me if you're not sure what to do next.
  set knows_fox
  choice What's the woven band? -> mummo_band
  choice I'll go. -> end

node mummo_band
  Mummo: My mother wove the fox's run into it, so we would never forget.
  Mummo: Every night it runs from snow-figure to snow-figure on the fell: a bear, a hare, an owl, over and over.
  Mummo: And from the owl, it leaps up into the sky.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the lake

node look_hole
  Vilja: A fishing hole cut in the ice, skinned over with new ice. Somebody fished here before the dark came.

node take_hole
  Vilja: It's a hole.

node look_fells
  Vilja: The fells roll away to the edge of the world, white under the stars. Somewhere up there, the fox is hiding.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the forest

node look_cabin
  Vilja: A trapper's cabin, its roof bowed under the snow. Pelts hang drying under the eaves.

node use_cabin
  Vilja: The door's barred. Jussi's standing right outside, though.

node look_snares
  Vilja: Wire snares, hung on the cabin wall: three on the wall and two on the peg by the door.

node take_snares
  Jussi: Hands off! I'm counting those.

node look_prints
  Vilja: Paw prints, glowing faintly, leading up toward the fell. The fox came this way!

node look_drifts
  Vilja: The path to the fell top, buried in drifts as deep as I am tall.

node walk_drifts
  Vilja: I'd sink to my waist in this snow. Mummo has snowshoes I could borrow.

node look_jussi
  Vilja: A trapper in a wolfskin coat, counting snares on his fingers and losing count.

node take_jussi
  Jussi: Oi! I'm not one of my pelts.

node talk_jussi
  [puukko] Jussi: Go on, then. Up the path to the fell top, and mind the fox's leg.
  [puukko] end
  [!met_jussi] Jussi: Who's that? A girl, out alone in this dark?
  [!met_jussi] Vilja: I'm looking for the fire fox. Mummo said you'd know where it is.
  [!met_jussi] Jussi: The fox... I set my snares on the fell top. I only wanted to catch a hare for my supper.
  [!met_jussi] Jussi: But it was the fox that got caught. It tore loose and ran off with my snare still on its leg. Poor thing.
  set met_jussi
  Jussi: I'm counting my snares to see how many are left. Three on the wall, and two on the peg by the door.
  Jussi: Numbers make my head hurt. How many is that altogether?
  jump jussi_count

node jussi_count
  choice Four -> count_wrong
  choice Five -> count_right
  choice Six -> count_wrong

node count_wrong
  Jussi: Hmm, is it? Let me count on my fingers. Three on the wall... and two on the peg...
  Vilja: Let's count again.
  jump jussi_count

node count_right
  Vilja: Three and two make five!
  Jussi: Five! I set out six this morning, so one is missing: the one on the fox's leg.
  Jussi: Here, take my puukko, my knife, and cut the wire off. Be careful: it's sharp.
  give puukko
  Jussi: The fox's den is in the rocks on the fell top. Up the path, past my cabin. Look out for its fur.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the fell top

node fell_enter
  [!seen_fell] Vilja: The top of the fell. Up here the wind has shaped the snow-laden spruces into figures.
  [!seen_fell] Vilja: That one is a hare. That one an owl. And that one a bear, rearing up.
  set seen_fell

node look_hare
  Vilja: A spruce so heavy with frozen snow that it looks like a hare, sitting up with its ears back.

node look_owl
  Vilja: A tall spruce wrapped in rime: an owl, its wings folded.

node look_bear
  Vilja: A great spruce bowed under the snow: a bear, rearing up on its hind legs.

node take_hare
  Vilja: It's a tree under half a ton of snow.

node take_owl
  jump take_hare

node take_bear
  jump take_hare

node look_snare
  Vilja: A torn snare. Caught in the wire: a tuft of fur that glows like an ember.

node take_snare
  Vilja: I pull the tuft of fur from the wire. It's still warm!
  set took_fur
  give fur
  Vilja: Now to find the fox. Its den must be in those rocks.

node look_fur
  Vilja: The fox's fur, glowing softly. It smells of smoke and frost.

node look_sky
  [!sky_lit] Vilja: Stars, and nothing else. The sky has never looked so empty.
  [sky_lit] Vilja: The whole sky is burning.

node look_fox
  [!run_1] Vilja: The fox, blazing like a bonfire, waiting by the bear. It keeps looking back at me.
  [run_1] Vilja: The fox, blazing by the hare, looking back at me. Waiting to know where next.

node talk_fox
  Vilja: Run, little fox! Which way do you go?
  Vilja: It only looks at me. Mummo's band shows the way: a bear, a hare, an owl.

# The fox's run, from the bear: the hare, then the owl, as Mummo's band goes. A wrong step
# and it trots back to the bear to start again.

node walk_hare
  [!fox_lit] jump look_hare
  [run_1] jump run_wrong
  set run_1
  Vilja: The fox streaks across the snow to the hare and circles it. Sparks fly up behind it!
  Vilja: And after the hare comes...?

node walk_owl
  [!fox_lit] jump look_owl
  [run_1] jump run_owl
  jump run_wrong

node walk_bear
  [!fox_lit] jump look_bear
  [run_1] jump run_wrong
  Vilja: The fox is already at the bear. Where does it run next?

node run_wrong
  clear run_1
  Vilja: The fox stops short and trots back to the bear to wait.
  Vilja: Mummo's band goes: a bear, a hare, an owl. The fox is at the bear, so next comes the hare.

node run_owl
  set sky_lit
  Vilja: The fox races up the owl-spruce, round and round, and leaps from the top of it into the sky!
  Vilja: Its tail sweeps across the dark, and the sparks... the sparks catch.
  Vilja: Green. Violet. Rose. The whole sky is burning!
  choice Whistle at the lights -> whistle
  choice Just watch -> watch

node whistle
  Vilja: I purse my lips to whistle...
  Vilja: No. Mummo says never whistle at the lights. I'll just watch.
  jump watch

node watch
  Vilja: Far off, the reindeer bells. The herd is coming home by the light.
  Vilja: Run, little fox. Run all night long.
  win

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the den

node den_enter
  [!fox_out] Vilja: It's dark under the rocks. Something glows faintly at the back.

node look_roots
  Vilja: Spruce roots, hanging through the roof of the den like an old man's beard.

node look_tail
  Vilja: A fox's tail, glowing faintly like an ember under ash. It twitches. The fox is hiding!

node talk_tail
  Vilja: Shh. I won't hurt you. I've come to help.
  Vilja: The tail curls away into the dark. It's too frightened. Maybe its own fur would help.

node take_tail
  Vilja: It's too frightened to let me near. Maybe its own fur would help.

node use_tail
  jump take_tail

node use_fur_tail
  Vilja: I lay the glowing tuft at the back of the den and wait.
  Vilja: A nose. A grey face. The fox creeps out to sniff at its own fur.
  remove fur
  set fox_out
  Vilja: There's the snare wire, round its leg. Jussi's puukko will cut it.

node use_puukko_tail
  jump take_tail

node use_ember_tail
  jump take_tail

node look_grey
  [!fox_free] Vilja: A fox, thin and grey as ash. Its tail hangs dull and cold. And there, round its leg: Jussi's snare wire.
  [fox_free] Vilja: Free of the wire, but so cold. Its tail is grey as ash. It needs fire.

node take_grey
  Vilja: It's frightened enough already.

node talk_grey
  [!fox_free] Vilja: There now. Let me get that wire off your leg.
  [fox_free] Vilja: You're shivering. My ember will warm your tail.

node use_puukko_grey
  [fox_free] Vilja: The wire's already off.
  [fox_free] end
  Vilja: Easy... easy. I slip the knife under the wire and cut.
  Vilja: The fox flinches, but it lets me. The wire falls away!
  set fox_free
  Vilja: But it's shivering. Its tail is cold as ash. Mummo's ember will warm it.

node use_ember_grey
  [!fox_free] Vilja: First the wire has to come off its leg. Jussi's puukko will cut it.
  [fox_free] jump fox_kindle

node fox_kindle
  Vilja: I open the box, blow on the ember, and hold it to the fox's tail.
  Vilja: A spark. Then another. Then its tail bursts into flame: gold, and red, and green!
  remove ember
  set fox_lit
  goto fell 196
  Vilja: It shot out of the den like a falling star!
  Vilja: It's stopped by the bear-spruce. It's looking back at me, waiting.
  Vilja: It's forgotten the way. Mummo's band shows it: a bear, a hare, an owl.
