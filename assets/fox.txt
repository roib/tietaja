# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the camp

node intro
  Vilja: Three nights now, and not one flicker in the sky.
  Vilja: Mummo sent word that the fox has stopped running. I came all the way north to find out what that means.

node look_kota
  Vilja: A kota of birch poles and turf, half buried in snow. Smoke curls out of the top, smelling of birch and dried fish.

node take_kota
  Vilja: It's a house, Vilja.

node use_kota
  Vilja: It's warm in there, but the fox won't be.

node look_snowshoes
  Vilja: Snowshoes, leaning against the kota. Birch frames laced with reindeer sinew.

node take_snowshoes
  [!knows_fox] Mummo: Those are mine, child! You'll ask an old woman first.
  [knows_fox] Vilja: Mummo's snowshoes. With these I won't sink to my waist on the fell.
  [knows_fox] give snowshoes

node look_band
  Vilja: A band of red and white wool, hung from the drying rack, woven with little figures.
  Vilja: A bear, then a hare. A bear, then two hares. A bear, then three hares. Then a bear...
  Vilja: ...and there the weave is frayed away.

node take_band
  Mummo: Leave that be. My mother wove it, and her mother taught her.

node look_fire
  Vilja: Mummo's fire. Birch logs, burning low and steady. The only warm light for miles.

node use_fire
  Vilja: I hold my hands to it for a moment. Better.

node take_fire
  Vilja: Not with my bare hands. I'd need something to carry an ember in.

node use_box_fire
  Vilja: I scoop a glowing ember into Mummo's birch-bark box and close the lid. It's warm against my mittens.
  remove box
  give ember
  Mummo: Carry it carefully. That's a piece of my hearth, and it has never once gone out.

node look_reindeer
  [!sky_lit] Vilja: Mummo's herd, huddled together in the dark. They keep lifting their heads to sniff the wind. The wolf is out there somewhere.
  [sky_lit] Vilja: The reindeer stand in the green light, calm again.

node take_reindeer
  Vilja: I think it would object.

node talk_reindeer
  Vilja: Hush now. The lights will come back. I promise.

node look_mummo
  Vilja: Mummo has herded reindeer on these fells for sixty winters. She keeps glancing up at the empty sky.

node take_mummo
  Mummo: Ha! I'm not a sack of flour.

node talk_mummo
  [!knows_fox] jump mummo_tale
  Mummo: Well, child?
  jump mummo_menu

node mummo_tale
  Mummo: Vilja! You came. Sit by the fire, quick. You've seen it? The sky?
  Vilja: It's so dark. Not one light, the whole way here.
  Mummo: Revontulet. The fox fires. Every winter night of my life, the fox has run across the fells, and its tail has swept the snow up into sparks.
  Mummo: The sparks fly up and burn in the sky. That's all the lights are.
  Mummo: But three nights ago it stopped running. Now the herd strays in the dark, and there's a wolf about.
  Vilja: Why would the fox stop?
  Mummo: Something has hurt it, or frightened it. Find it, child. You've always had a way with wild things.
  Mummo: And mind: whatever you do, never whistle at the sky. Whistle at the lights, and they come down for you.
  set knows_fox
  Mummo: Take my snowshoes if you're going up the fell. And take this birch-bark box. If you find the fox cold, it will need fire.
  give box
  jump mummo_menu

node mummo_menu
  choice Where would the fox be? -> mummo_where
  choice What's the woven band? -> mummo_band
  [knows_snare] choice The fox is caught in Jussi's snare! -> mummo_snare
  [fox_free !fox_lit] choice The fox is free, but it's cold. -> mummo_cold
  choice I'll go and look. -> end

node mummo_where
  Mummo: It runs on the fell top, past the snow-figures the wind has made of the spruces.
  Mummo: Jussi the trapper lives in the forest below the fell. If anything with fur has been up there, he'll know about it.

node mummo_band
  Mummo: My mother wove the fox's run into it, so we would never forget.
  Mummo: Every night the fox runs past the snow-figures on the fell, and every night a little farther than the night before.
  Mummo: And when it has run far enough, it goes to the owl, and the owl sends it up into the sky.
  Mummo: The end of the band has worn away. But I always knew the run by heart. Until now, when it matters.

node mummo_snare
  Mummo: That Jussi! Wire on its leg, in the cold. No wonder it can't run.
  Mummo: You'll need something sharp to cut it.

node mummo_cold
  Mummo: Then give it fire, child. There's an ember in my hearth waiting for you.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the lake

node look_hole
  [!fished] Vilja: A fishing hole cut in the ice, skinned over with new ice. Somebody fished here before the dark came.
  [fished] Vilja: The hole is freezing over again already.

node take_hole
  Vilja: It's a hole.

node use_hole
  Vilja: I'd need a fishing line.

node look_line
  Vilja: An old fishing line, frozen into the ice next to the hole.

node take_line
  Vilja: I work it loose from the ice. There's still a hook on the end.
  set took_line
  give line

node use_line_hole
  Vilja: I break the thin ice and let the line down into the black water.
  Vilja: ...
  Vilja: A tug! A fat whitefish, silver as the moon.
  remove line
  give fish
  set fished

node look_fells
  Vilja: The fells roll away to the edge of the world, white under the stars. Somewhere up there, the fox is hiding.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the forest

node look_cabin
  Vilja: A trapper's cabin, its roof bowed under the snow. Pelts hang drying under the eaves.

node use_cabin
  Vilja: The door's barred. Jussi doesn't seem the trusting kind.

node look_snares
  Vilja: Wire snares, dozens of them, hung in bundles on the cabin wall.

node take_snares
  Jussi: Hands off! I've counted those. Or I've tried to.

node look_prints
  Vilja: Paw prints, leading up toward the fell. Each one still holds a faint glow, like ash that hasn't quite gone out.

node look_drifts
  Vilja: The path to the fell top, buried in drifts as deep as I am tall.

node walk_drifts
  Vilja: I'd sink to my waist in this snow. I need snowshoes.

node look_jussi
  Vilja: A trapper in a wolfskin coat, counting snares on his fingers and losing count.

node take_jussi
  Jussi: Oi! Try that again and I'll set a snare for you.

node talk_jussi
  [!met_jussi] jump jussi_meet
  Jussi: You again.
  jump jussi_menu

node jussi_meet
  Jussi: Who's that? A girl, out alone in this dark? Mind the snares.
  Vilja: I'm looking for the fire fox.
  Jussi: Hah! So is every fool in Lapland. But I'm the one who nearly had it.
  Jussi: Caught it in a snare on the fell top, three nights back. Its tail lit up the whole forest. A pelt like that would make a man as rich as a king.
  Jussi: But it tore loose and ran off, and then the dark came down. So I'm bringing my snares in.
  set met_jussi
  jump jussi_menu

node jussi_menu
  [!knows_snare] choice Have you brought them all in? -> jussi_count
  [!asked_hungry] choice You look hungry. -> jussi_hungry
  [!puukko] choice Could I borrow your knife? -> jussi_knife
  choice You hurt the fox! -> jussi_hurt
  choice Goodbye. -> end

node jussi_count
  Jussi: That's what I'm trying to work out. Numbers were never my strong suit.
  Jussi: The first day I set three snares. And every day after, I set twice as many as the day before. Four days of setting, I did.
  Jussi: I've brought back forty-four. So, clever girl: how many did I set?
  choice Twenty-four. -> count_24
  choice Thirty-six. -> count_36
  choice Forty-five. -> count_45
  choice Forty-eight. -> count_48

node count_24
  Jussi: Twenty-four? I've got forty-four in my hands already! You're worse at sums than I am.
  jump jussi_menu

node count_36
  Jussi: Thirty-six? But I've brought back more than that. Count it again.
  jump jussi_menu

node count_48
  Jussi: Forty-eight? Three, then six... no, that's too many. I'm sure of it.
  jump jussi_menu

node count_45
  Vilja: Three the first day, then six, then twelve, then twenty-four. Forty-five.
  Jussi: Forty-five! Then one's missing.
  Jussi: The one on the fell top. The fox tore loose... and took my snare with it. The wire must still be on its leg.
  Vilja: The poor thing. That's why it can't run.
  Jussi: Then it won't have gone far. It'll be holed up in its den, in the rocks on the fell top.
  set knows_snare
  jump jussi_menu

node jussi_hungry
  Jussi: Hungry? I haven't eaten in two days. Can't hunt in this dark. Can't see my own boots.
  Jussi: Bring me a fish and I'd trade you nearly anything.
  set asked_hungry
  jump jussi_menu

node jussi_knife
  Jussi: My puukko? My grandfather's knife? Not for nothing, girl.
  [asked_hungry] Jussi: Now if you had a fish...
  jump jussi_menu

node jussi_hurt
  Jussi: Hurt it? I'd have made it famous! Every fur trader from here to Tornio would know my name.
  [knows_fox] Vilja: Without the fox there are no lights, Jussi. Nobody will be trading anything in this dark.
  [knows_fox] Jussi: ...Hm. I never thought of it like that.
  jump jussi_menu

node use_fish_jussi
  Jussi: A fish! A fat one, too. My stomach thanks you.
  Jussi: A fair trade, then. My puukko. Mind, it's sharp enough to cut through a snare wire.
  remove fish
  give puukko
  set fed_jussi

node use_line_jussi
  Jussi: A line's no use to me without a fish on it.

  set seen_fell

node look_hare

  Vilja: A great spruce bowed under the snow: a bear, rearing up on its hind legs.

  jump take_hare

  Vilja: A snare, its peg torn half out of the snow. Caught in the broken wire: a tuft of fur that glows like an ember.

  Vilja: I pull the tuft of fur from the wire. It's still warm.
  set took_fur

node look_fur
  Vilja: The fox's fur, glowing softly. It smells of smoke and frost.

node look_sky
  [!sky_lit] Vilja: Stars, and nothing else. The sky has never looked so empty.
  [sky_lit] Vilja: The whole sky is burning.

node look_fox
  [!run_1] Vilja: The fox, blazing like a bonfire, waiting by the bear. It keeps looking back at me.
  [run_1] Vilja: The fox, blazing by the hare, looking back at me. Waiting to know where next.

node talk_fox
  Vilja: Run, little fox! Which way do you go?
  Vilja: It only looks at me. It's forgotten the way. Mummo's band tells the way, but the end is frayed.

# The fox's run, from the bear: as the band goes on, a bear and then four hares, and when it
# has run far enough, the owl. A wrong step and it trots back to the bear to start over.

node walk_hare
  [!fox_lit] jump look_hare
  [run_4] jump run_wrong
  [run_3] jump run_hare_4
  [run_2] jump run_hare_3
  [run_1] jump run_hare_2
  jump run_hare_1

node walk_owl
  [!fox_lit] jump look_owl
  [run_4] jump run_owl
  jump run_wrong

node walk_bear
  [!fox_lit] jump look_bear
  [run_1] jump run_wrong
  Vilja: The fox is already at the bear. Where does it run next?

node run_hare_1
  set run_1
  Vilja: The fox streaks across the snow to the hare and circles it once. Sparks fly up behind it.

node run_hare_2
  set run_2
  Vilja: Around the hare again! Twice now. The sparks hang in the air a little longer.

node run_hare_3
  set run_3
  Vilja: Three times around the hare. The snow is glowing where it runs.

node run_hare_4
  set run_4
  Vilja: Four times! The fox's tail is so bright now I can hardly look at it. It's waiting for something...

node run_wrong
  clear run_1
  clear run_2
  clear run_3
  clear run_4
  Vilja: The fox stops short. Its sparks scatter and go out, and it trots back to the bear to wait.
  Vilja: That wasn't the way. Mummo's band: a bear, then one hare, a bear, then two... it runs a little farther every night.

node run_owl
  set sky_lit
  Vilja: The fox races up the owl-spruce, round and round, and leaps from the top of it into the sky!
  Vilja: Its tail sweeps across the dark, and the sparks... the sparks catch.
  Vilja: Green. Violet. Rose. The whole sky is burning!
  choice Whistle at the lights -> whistle
  choice Just watch -> watch

node whistle
  Vilja: I purse my lips to whistle...
  Vilja: No. Never whistle at the sky. The lights dip low over me, as if they heard me think it.
  jump watch

node watch
  Vilja: Far off, the reindeer bells. The herd is coming home by the light.
  Vilja: And somewhere down in the forest, somebody yells. I think the lights came down very low over Jussi's cabin.
  win

node take_grey
  Vilja: It's frightened enough already.

  Vilja: There now. Nobody's going to hurt you any more.

node use_puukko_grey
  [fox_free] Vilja: The wire's already off.
  [!fox_free] Vilja: Easy... easy. I slip the knife under the wire and cut.
  [!fox_free] Vilja: The fox flinches, but it lets me. The wire falls away.
  [!fox_free] set fox_free
  Vilja: But it's shivering. Its tail is cold as ash. It needs fire.

node use_box_grey
  Vilja: The box is empty. I need an ember from a fire.

node use_fish_grey
  Vilja: It sniffs the fish, but it doesn't eat. It's not hunger that's wrong with it.

  [!fox_free] Vilja: Not while that wire is still biting its leg. It would only bolt.
  [fox_free] jump fox_kindle

node fox_kindle
  Vilja: I open the box, blow on the ember, and hold it to the fox's tail.
  Vilja: A spark. Then another. Then its tail bursts into flame: gold, and red, and green!
  remove ember
  set fox_lit
  goto fell 196
  Vilja: It shot out of the den like a falling star!
  Vilja: It's stopped by the bear-spruce. It's looking back at me, waiting.
  Vilja: Three nights in the dark... it's forgotten the way. I have to show it, the way Mummo's band tells it.
