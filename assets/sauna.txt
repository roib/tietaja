# clean water for the stones, a fresh birch vihta, and a kind word. They can be given in
# any order; when all three are given, the steam turns sweet again. A tale for young
# players: few steps, the important things sparkle, and Kyllikki always says what's left.
# The file format is described at the top of story.txt.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the three gifts
#
# After each gift: if all three are given, the finale; if not, what's still missing.

node check_gifts
  [gift_water gift_vihta gift_word] jump finale
  [!gift_water] Vilja: Still to do: clean water for the stones, from the lake.
  [!gift_vihta] Vilja: Still to do: a vihta of birch twigs, from the old birch in the grove.
  [!gift_word] Vilja: Still to do: a kind word for the keeper.

node finale
  Vilja: Water, a vihta, and a kind word. Now the song Grandfather taught me...
  Vilja: (singing) Ukko, father in the sky, send us honey-steam, sweet steam through the stones...
  Vilja: (with the ladle) ...soft as a mother's breath, warm as summer earth!
  Keeper: Ahh.
  Keeper: Gently, child. Gently, as I taught you.
  Vilja: Grandfather?
  Keeper: Mind the stones. And mind your manners in here.
  Kyllikki: (from outside) Now THAT is löyly!
  win

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the village

node intro
  Vilja: Saturday. Kyllikki has been heating the sauna since noon.
  Vilja: But the smoke coming out of it smells... cross. Kyllikki will know what's wrong.

node look_sauna
  [!gift_word] Vilja: Grandfather built it with his own hands. The smoke seeping out of it looks sulky.
  [gift_word] Vilja: The smoke drifts out of it gently now, like a sigh.

node use_sauna
  Vilja: The door's round the front, on the left.

node look_house
  Vilja: Kyllikki's house. Warm light in the window: at least that fire's happy.

node use_house
  Vilja: Kyllikki doesn't like people in her house when she's not in it. Or when she is.

node look_fire
  Vilja: The fire I sang back to life. Still burning.

node take_fire
  Vilja: I've learned that lesson.

node talk_fire
  Vilja: Good evening, fire. You stay right there.

node look_stump
  Vilja: Kyllikki's chopping stump.

node look_kyllikki
  Vilja: Kyllikki, wrapped in her sauna towel, waiting.

node take_kyllikki
  Vilja: She'd hit me with her stick. Again.

node talk_kyllikki
  [!knows_gifts] jump kyllikki_gifts
  [gift_water gift_vihta gift_word] Kyllikki: All done? Then go and listen to the steam!
  [gift_water gift_vihta gift_word] end
  Kyllikki: Well? How are the keeper's gifts coming along?
  [!gift_water !water] Kyllikki: Water first: take the bucket from the sauna and fill it at the lake.
  [water !gift_water] Kyllikki: You've got the water. Throw it on the stove in the sauna.
  [!gift_vihta !vihta] Kyllikki: The vihta: break some twigs off the old birch, deep in the forest.
  [vihta !gift_vihta] Kyllikki: You've got a vihta. Lay it on the sauna benches for the keeper.
  [!gift_word] Kyllikki: And the kind word: talk to the keeper, the glowing eyes under the benches. Say sorry nicely.
  choice Who is the keeper? -> kyllikki_keeper
  choice What upset it? -> kyllikki_upset
  choice I'll go and do it. -> end

node kyllikki_gifts
  Kyllikki: There you are! But the sauna's no good tonight, child. The keeper is upset.
  Kyllikki: Every sauna has a keeper. It lives under the benches and looks after the steam.
  Kyllikki: When it's upset, the steam stings like nettles.
  Vilja: How do we cheer it up?
  Kyllikki: Bring it three gifts.
  Kyllikki: One: clean water for the stones. Take the bucket from the sauna and fill it at the lake.
  Kyllikki: Two: a vihta. Break some leafy twigs off the old birch, deep in the forest.
  Kyllikki: Three: a kind word. Talk to the keeper and say sorry nicely.
  Vilja: Water, a vihta and a kind word. I can do that!
  Kyllikki: Ask me any time if you forget.
  set knows_gifts
  choice Who is the keeper? -> kyllikki_keeper
  choice What upset it? -> kyllikki_upset
  choice I'll go and do it. -> end

node kyllikki_keeper
  Kyllikki: The keeper. Saunatonttu, some say. It was here before any of us.
  Kyllikki: Some say a sauna's keeper is whoever built it, staying on after.
  Kyllikki: The sauna is the keeper's home, child, and we're its guests. No shouting, no squabbling, no rude words.

node kyllikki_upset
  Kyllikki: That cheeky magpie flew in and stole my spindle, right out of the sauna.
  Kyllikki: I chased it round and round the stones, shouting my head off.
  Kyllikki: Not very good manners in somebody's home, was it?

node use_water_kyllikki
  Kyllikki: That's for the stones, not for me. Throw it on the stove in the sauna.

node use_bucket_kyllikki
  Kyllikki: Empty! Fill it at the lake, down the path on the left.

node use_vihta_kyllikki
  Kyllikki: Lovely and leafy! It's not for me, though. Lay it on the sauna benches for the keeper.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the sauna

node sauna_enter
  [!been_in_sauna] Vilja: (coughing) The steam stings like nettles!
  [!been_in_sauna] Keeper: Hssssss!
  [!been_in_sauna !knows_gifts] Vilja: Two eyes are glowing under the benches. I'd better ask Kyllikki what to do.
  set been_in_sauna

node look_kiuas
  [!gift_water] Vilja: The kiuas: a heap of stones over the fire. You throw water on them to make steam.
  [gift_water] Vilja: The stones are hot and happy.

node use_kiuas
  [!water] Vilja: I need clean water to throw on the stones. There's a bucket by the stove.
  [water] jump use_water_kiuas

node take_kiuas
  Vilja: They're hot stones. Very hot stones.

node use_water_kiuas
  Vilja: (filling the ladle) Clean lake water, for your stones.
  remove water
  set gift_water
  Vilja: Tssssss... The steam is softer already.
  jump check_gifts

node look_benches
  [!gift_vihta] Vilja: The benches. The glowing eyes are underneath.
  [gift_vihta] Vilja: The vihta lies on the upper bench, ready for the keeper.

node use_vihta_benches
  Vilja: (laying the vihta on the upper bench) For you, keeper. Fresh from the old birch.
  remove vihta
  set gift_vihta
  Keeper: Mm?
  jump check_gifts

node use_vihta_keeper
  jump use_vihta_benches

node talk_benches
  jump talk_keeper

node look_keeper
  [!gift_word] Vilja: Two eyes, glowing like coals, under the benches. They look very cross.
  [gift_word] Vilja: The eyes under the benches are half closed, like a contented cat's.

node talk_keeper
  [gift_word] Keeper: Mm.
  [gift_word] end
  Keeper: Hssss!
  jump keeper_choices

node keeper_choices
  choice Say sorry nicely -> sorry_nicely
  choice Hiss back at it -> sorry_hiss
  choice Tell it to cheer up -> sorry_cheer

node sorry_hiss
  Vilja: Hssssss!
  Keeper: HSSSSSS!!
  Vilja: Oops. That made it crosser. Let's try something kinder.
  jump keeper_choices

node sorry_cheer
  Vilja: Oh, cheer up!
  Keeper: Hmph.
  Vilja: Hmm. That's not very kind when someone's upset. Maybe I should say sorry.
  jump keeper_choices

node sorry_nicely
  Vilja: I'm sorry, keeper. This is your home, and we were noisy in it.
  Vilja: We'll be gentle and quiet in here from now on. I promise.
  Keeper: ...
  Keeper: Mm.
  set gift_word
  Vilja: The eyes have gone soft. It's listening!
  jump check_gifts

node take_keeper
  Vilja: I am NOT putting my hand under there.

node look_window
  Vilja: The evening sun is going down over the lake.

node look_ladle
  Vilja: A wooden ladle for throwing water on the stones.

node take_ladle
  Vilja: The ladle belongs to the sauna. I'll use it here.

node look_bucket
  Vilja: The sauna bucket. It's empty: just a little old ash in the bottom.

node take_bucket
  give bucket
  set took_bucket
  Vilja: The bucket! I'll fill it with clean water at the lake.

node use_bucket_kiuas
  Vilja: It's empty. I need to fill it at the lake first.

node look_vihta
  Vilja: The vihta, waiting on the upper bench for the keeper.

node take_vihta
  Vilja: It's the keeper's now.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the grove

node look_birch
  Vilja: The ancient birch. Its leafy twigs are the finest in the forest.

node take_birch
  [took_twigs] Vilja: I have my vihta. Taking more would be greedy.
  [took_twigs] end
  Vilja: (breaking off leafy twigs) Thank you, old birch.
  Vilja: (tying them with her hair ribbon) There: a vihta for the keeper!
  give vihta
  set took_twigs
  Vilja: Now to lay it on the sauna benches.

  jump take_birch

  Vilja: The offering stone, soft with moss.
