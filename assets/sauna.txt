# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the village

node intro
  Vilja: Saturday. Kyllikki has been heating the sauna since noon.
  Vilja: But the smoke coming out of it smells... angry. Is that possible?

node look_sauna
  [!apologized] Vilja: Grandfather built it with his own hands. The smoke seeping out of it looks sulky.
  [apologized] Vilja: The smoke drifts out of it gently now, like a sigh.

node use_sauna
  Vilja: The door's round the front.

node look_house
  Vilja: Kyllikki's house. Warm light in the window: at least that fire's happy.

node use_house
  Vilja: Kyllikki doesn't like people in her house when she's not in it. Or when she is.

node look_fire
  Vilja: The fire I sang back to life. Still burning.

node take_fire
  Vilja: I've learned that lesson.

node talk_fire
  Vilja: Good evening, fire. You stay right there.

node look_stump
  Vilja: Kyllikki's chopping stump.

node look_kyllikki
  Vilja: Kyllikki, wrapped in her sauna towel, waiting.

node take_kyllikki
  Vilja: She'd hit me with her stick. Again.

node talk_kyllikki
  [kyllikki_in_sauna] jump kyllikki_apology
  [!met_kyllikki] Kyllikki: There you are! The sauna's been heating since noon. Go in, go in.
  [!met_kyllikki] Kyllikki: Young bones first, so I can have the good steam after.
  [met_kyllikki] Kyllikki: Well? Is it sweet in there yet?
  set met_kyllikki
  jump kyllikki_menu

node kyllikki_menu
  choice [felt_bite !knows_amends] The steam in there bites. Something's wrong. -> kyllikki_bite
  choice [knows_amends !knows_culprit] How do I make amends? -> kyllikki_amends
  choice [knows_culprit water vihta] The sauna's ready. Will you come and say sorry? -> kyllikki_ready
  choice [knows_culprit !water] Will you apologise to the keeper? -> kyllikki_refuses
  choice [knows_culprit water !vihta] Will you apologise to the keeper? -> kyllikki_refuses
  choice Who looks after the sauna? -> kyllikki_keeper
  choice Goodbye. -> end

node kyllikki_bite
  Kyllikki: Bites? Löyly doesn't bite. Not unless...
  Kyllikki: Oh, no. Somebody's upset the keeper.
  Kyllikki: Every sauna has one, child. It lives under the benches and minds the steam.
  Kyllikki: Upset it, and the steam turns sour. Leave it long enough and it'll burn the sauna down.
  Vilja: How do I make it better?
  Kyllikki: Clean water for the stones. A fresh vihta, left for the keeper to bathe with.
  Kyllikki: And an apology from whoever offended it. Done properly, mind.
  set knows_amends
  jump kyllikki_menu

node kyllikki_amends
  Kyllikki: Clean water for the stones, from the lake. A fresh vihta of birch twigs.
  Kyllikki: And whoever was rude in there has to say sorry. Find out who.
  jump kyllikki_menu

node kyllikki_keeper
  Kyllikki: The keeper. Saunatonttu, some say. It was here before any of us.
  Kyllikki: Some say a sauna's keeper is whoever built it, staying on after.
  Kyllikki: Behave in the sauna as you would in church. No shouting, no fighting, no rude words.
  jump kyllikki_menu

node kyllikki_refuses
  Kyllikki: Me? Apologise to a puff of steam?
  Kyllikki: ...Fine. But I'm not going in until the sauna's ready. Water and a vihta, and then we'll talk.
  jump kyllikki_menu

node kyllikki_ready
  Kyllikki: Water and a vihta. You have been busy.
  Kyllikki: Fine. I'll come. But I'm not crawling under any bench.
  set kyllikki_in_sauna
  Vilja: She's gone ahead to the sauna.

node use_feather_kyllikki
  [!heard_magpie] Kyllikki: A magpie feather. So? There are magpies everywhere.
  [!heard_magpie] end
  Kyllikki: A magpie feather. What of it?
  Vilja: It was under the sauna benches. And the magpie on the path says... words. In your voice.
  Kyllikki: ...
  Kyllikki: I may have said one or two things when that thief stole my spindle.
  Kyllikki: In the sauna. Loudly. While I was chasing it round the stones.
  Vilja: Kyllikki!
  Kyllikki: The bird repeated them. That's what offended the keeper, not me.
  Vilja: The bird only said them because you did.
  Kyllikki: Hmph.
  set knows_culprit

node use_twigs_kyllikki
  Kyllikki: Birch twigs! Good ones, from the old birch. Give them here.
  Kyllikki: (binding them with a twist of yarn) There. A vihta fit for the keeper.
  remove twigs
  give vihta

node use_water_kyllikki
  Kyllikki: That's for the stones, not for me. I washed last Saturday.

node use_bucket_kyllikki
  Kyllikki: Ash! Tip that out somewhere and fetch clean water from the lake.

node use_coin_kyllikki
  Kyllikki: Silver? Keep it. Silver's for the spirits, not for old women.

node use_vihta_kyllikki
  Kyllikki: It's not for me, it's for the keeper. Leave it on the bench in there.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the sauna

node sauna_enter
  [!been_in_sauna] Vilja: (coughing) The smoke's thick in here. And it stings.
  [!been_in_sauna] Vilja: Something under the benches is watching me.
  set been_in_sauna

node look_kiuas
  [!felt_bite] Vilja: The kiuas. A heap of stones over the fire, hot enough to make good steam.
  [felt_bite !apologized] Vilja: The stones are hot, but the steam they give is sour.
  [apologized] Vilja: The stones are hot and ready.

node use_kiuas
  [!felt_bite] Vilja: Just a ladle of water to see...
  [!felt_bite] Vilja: Ow! The steam bit me! It stings like nettles!
  [!felt_bite] Keeper: Hsssss!
  [!felt_bite] set felt_bite
  [!felt_bite] end
  [!apologized] Vilja: I'm not throwing any more water on that until the keeper's calmer.
  [apologized] Vilja: It needs clean water. From the lake.

node take_kiuas
  Vilja: They're hot stones. Very hot stones.

node look_benches
  [!vihta_left] Vilja: The benches. The upper one gets the best steam.
  [vihta_left] Vilja: The vihta lies on the upper bench, waiting for the keeper.

node use_vihta_benches
  [!apologized] Vilja: Not yet. The keeper won't take a gift from someone it's angry with.
  [!apologized] end
  Vilja: (laying the vihta on the upper bench) For you, keeper. Fresh from the old birch.
  remove vihta
  set vihta_left
  Keeper: ...

node use_twigs_benches
  Vilja: Loose twigs won't do. They need binding into a proper vihta.

node look_keeper
  [!apologized] Vilja: Two eyes, glowing like coals, under the benches. They don't blink when I look.
  [apologized] Vilja: The eyes under the benches are half closed, like a contented cat's.

node talk_keeper
  [!apologized] Vilja: Hello? I'm Vilja. Grandfather's...
  [!apologized] Keeper: Hssssss!
  [!apologized] Vilja: It's angry. Someone has to apologise to it. Properly.
  [apologized] Keeper: Mm.

node take_keeper
  Vilja: I am NOT putting my hand under there.

node use_feather_keeper
  Keeper: Hsss!
  Vilja: It knows that feather.

node look_window
  Vilja: The evening sun is going down over the lake.

node look_vent
  [!vent_open] Vilja: The räppänä, the smoke vent. Someone has slid it almost shut.
  [vent_open] Vilja: The vent's open. The smoke streams out into the evening.

node use_vent
  [vent_open] Vilja: It's open already.
  [vent_open] end
  Vilja: (sliding the board open) There.
  set vent_open
  Vilja: The smoke's thinning. I can see the ceiling again.

node take_vent
  jump use_vent

node look_beam
  [!vent_open] Vilja: There's something carved on the beam, but the smoke's too thick to read it.
  [!vent_open] end
  [!knows_loyly] Vilja: Grandfather's marks. He carved these when he built the sauna.
  [!knows_loyly] Vilja: Words, cut small, as if he didn't want just anyone to read them.
  [!knows_loyly] set knows_loyly
  Vilja: "Ukko, father in the sky, send us honey-steam, sweet steam through the stones..."
  Vilja: The words for löyly. The real ones.

node look_ladle
  Vilja: A wooden ladle for throwing water on the stones.

node take_ladle
  Vilja: The ladle belongs to the sauna. I'll use it here.

node look_bucket
  Vilja: The water bucket. Someone's filled it with ash.

node take_bucket
  give bucket
  set took_bucket
  Vilja: Full of ash. The keeper must have spoiled it. I'll need clean water.

node look_feather
  Vilja: A black feather, flashing blue and green. Half under the benches.

node take_feather
  give feather
  set took_feather
  Vilja: A magpie's tail feather. What was a magpie doing in here?

node use_bucket_kiuas
  Vilja: Ash on hot stones? The keeper would never forgive me.

node use_water_kiuas
  [!vihta_left] jump not_yet
  [!knows_loyly] Vilja: Plain water won't sweeten this steam. I need the right words.
  [!knows_loyly] end
  Vilja: (filling the ladle) Here goes.
  Vilja: (singing) Ukko, father in the sky, send us honey-steam, sweet steam through the stones...
  remove water
  Vilja: (throwing the water) ...soft as a mother's breath, warm as summer earth!
  Keeper: Ahh.
  Keeper: Gently, child. Gently, as I taught you.
  Vilja: Grandfather?
  Keeper: Mind the stones. And mind your tongue in here.
  Kyllikki: Now THAT is löyly.
  win

node not_yet
  [!apologized] Vilja: Not yet. It's still angry. Water alone won't calm it.
  [apologized !vihta_left] Vilja: The keeper should have its vihta first.

# Kyllikki in the sauna: the apology, which has to be said properly.
node kyllikki_apology
  [apologized] Kyllikki: Get on with it, child. My bones are cold.
  [apologized] end
  Kyllikki: Well? What do I say to a pair of eyes under a bench?
  jump apology_menu

node apology_menu
  choice Just say sorry. -> apology_short
  choice Forgive my tongue, keeper of this sauna. It won't happen again. -> apology_proper
  choice Say it was the magpie's fault. -> apology_magpie

node apology_short
  Kyllikki: Sorry.
  Keeper: Hssss!
  Kyllikki: It didn't like that.
  Vilja: Maybe a bit more... properly?
  jump apology_menu

node apology_magpie
  Kyllikki: It was the magpie that said it, you know. Mostly.
  Keeper: HSSSSS!
  Kyllikki: Alright, alright!
  jump apology_menu

node apology_proper
  Kyllikki: (clearing her throat) Forgive my tongue, keeper of this sauna.
  Kyllikki: It was a bad day and a worse bird. It won't happen again.
  Keeper: ...
  Keeper: Mm.
  Vilja: The eyes have gone soft. It's listening.
  set apologized

  [!gift_given] Vilja: No. The grove gives nothing for free. I need to leave a gift first.

  [took_twigs] Vilja: I have enough twigs. Taking more would be greedy.
